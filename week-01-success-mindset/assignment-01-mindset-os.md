# Assignment 1. What is something you believe to be true that most people around you would disagree with?

### Rules

* No "safe" answers.
* Must be your real belief (not copied from internet).
* Minimum 50 words.

**Hint:** What do you believe about career, money, learning, discipline, relationships, health, success, life, tech industry, etc. that most people don't agree with?

## Answer

I believe that getting a job is not the final goal of engineering education; becoming a person who can consistently solve real problems is more valuable in the long run. Many students around me focus mainly on marks, certificates, and getting placed in a reputed company. I believe that skills, practical experience, and the ability to learn independently create opportunities beyond a single placement season. A student who builds projects, understands failures, and improves every day can create more long-term value than someone who only prepares to pass examinations. I also believe that discipline is more important than motivation because motivation changes, but a consistent system keeps progress moving even on difficult days. Success is not just about being talented or lucky; it is about repeatedly doing useful work, learning from mistakes, and proving what we can do.

Vidhul's 2.0 version represented a professional who combined technical knowledge with practical execution, clear communication, leadership, and lifelong learning. ### Answer Format

**Yes**

## Answer

There have been situations where I took shortcuts in my learning or preparation instead of giving the task the time and effort it deserved. Sometimes I focused on completing an assignment or solving a problem quickly rather than understanding the concept deeply. This made me feel regret because I knew that finishing a task was not the same as genuinely learning something. I also felt stress when I had to face a similar problem later without having strong fundamentals. I want to improve by being honest about what I know, completing work properly, and taking responsibility for my learning.

# Reflection – Week 1

### Biggest insight I got about myself this week

My biggest insight is that I have the ability to learn and build difficult technical projects, but my progress depends on how consistently I execute my plans. I have explored several areas of technology and completed meaningful projects. I realized that I need to focus more on finishing what I start, documenting my work, and improving one skill at a time.

My biggest weakness is inconsistent execution. I sometimes begin learning multiple technologies or projects with enthusiasm but struggle to maintain the same level of focus over time.
